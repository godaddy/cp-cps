## GoDaddy CA Values

### Overview and Value Proposition

GoDaddy operates a distributed, resilient Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) engineered for high availability and low-latency certificate issuance and revocation. We provide automation via ACME and a documented SSL API that supports end-to-end certificate lifecycle operations. Our scale enables consistent, standards-aligned certificate management, while our broad customer reach expands the availability of trusted TLS across diverse platforms and geographies. We continuously invest in reliability engineering, automation, and telemetry to reduce operational risk and provide a predictable, stable service to internet users and developers.

### Security, Compliance, and Transparency

GoDaddy’s security program is built upon established industry frameworks, ensuring a robust and reliable foundation for protecting certificate operations. The program emphasizes continuous monitoring to proactively detect and address potential security threats. Vulnerability management is a core focus, with systematic processes in place to identify, assess, and remediate vulnerabilities across infrastructure and applications.

Secure software development practices are integrated throughout the lifecycle, supporting the creation and maintenance of resilient products and services. Additionally, dedicated security teams conduct regular assessments to validate the effectiveness of controls and identify opportunities for further risk reduction. These combined efforts support GoDaddy’s commitment to maintaining a secure, compliant, and trustworthy PKI environment for all users.

To ensure transparency, we maintain an incident response program that includes detection, triage, stakeholder notification, and public disclosure stages. Our incident management process includes root-cause analysis, taking corrective and preventive actions when necessary, and providing transparent post-incident communications via standard public channels.

Our certificate policy and certification practice statement (CP/CPS) is governed by a structured management process with version control, legal review, and a full review at least annually. We complete annual, independent WebTrust audits and CCADB self-assessments, publishing all artifacts in line with industry norms.

### Privacy and Data Protection

GoDaddy collects data required to validate certificate requests at the appropriate assurance level. Data processing is limited to CA purposes, and information is retained as required by policy and program rules. The CA adheres to the Starfield Technologies Privacy Policy, which governs data minimization, transparency, and user privacy protections. User information is kept private and is not shared with third-party vendors.

### Community Engagement and Ecosystem Contributions

We participate in the CA/Browser Forum and related standards discussions, contributing through working groups, public feedback, and sharing implementation experience with peers. We support ecosystem alignment on baseline requirements, incident handling best practices, and the modernization of certificate profiles and validation methods. When gaps are identified, we contribute enhancements to open-source linting and validation tools to strengthen conformance across the ecosystem. Furthermore, we are implementing a static Certificate Transparency (CT) log with the goal of qualifying as a trusted CT log provider to improve ecosystem resilience.

### Roadmap and Ongoing Commitments

Our roadmap centers on continuously improving automation across the certificate lifecycle to lower risk and enhance accuracy. A key initiative is the operationalization of new root generation to enable five-year root rotation. This strategy aligns with community goals of reliable issuance, robust validation, rapid revocation, and timely adoption of modern cryptographic profiles.

GoDaddy is committed to the ongoing improvement of its CA products, with an emphasis on customer automation, proactive monitoring, and overall certificate health. We maintain a cycle of continuous improvement across operations, policy, and security; adopt evolving standards promptly; communicate transparently with root programs; and responsibly deprecate legacy practices in accordance with industry consensus.
