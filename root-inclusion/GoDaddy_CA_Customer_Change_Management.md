## GoDaddy CA Customer and Change Management

### Public resources for upcoming changes

We provide publicly accessible help [articles](https://www.godaddy.com/help/ssl-certificates-1000006) that announce upcoming changes, outline timelines, and describe expected impacts. These resources are kept current as plans evolve to ensure all stakeholders have reliable guidance.

### Communications to existing subscribers

We communicate upcoming changes through multiple channels, including targeted email to account contacts, public help articles, in‑product notifications, and partner communications. Messages clearly state timelines, required customer actions, and any subscriber‑specific impacts.

### Maintaining accurate subscriber contacts

Customer contact information is maintained within account profiles, and customers can update details at any time.

### Gathering feedback on proposed industry changes

We collect input from customers who contact our support teams and through regular direct communications with industry partners, incorporating feedback into implementation approaches and timelines when relevant.
